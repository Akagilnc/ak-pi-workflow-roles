# Owner synthesis 006: remove generic raw retention from Recorder v1

After considering Judge-004, Reviewer-004, capacity evidence, and the owner's latest value judgment, v1 should become smaller rather than deepen a cold-store subsystem.

1. Supersede amendment 004's generic cold forensic stream and amendment 005's exceptional generic raw-session retention. Recorder v1 has no cold root, raw admission reasons/ranges, compression/chunking format, cold locator, retention policy, or second storage temperature.
2. Recorder captures raw private scratch only as needed to obtain the child outcome, extract the package-accepted typed Receipt/audit acceptance, perform bounded credential scanning/redaction, and assemble the small core. Routine tool calls, deltas, reads, and reasoning are execution exhaust. Required cleanup succeeds before atomic core promotion; cleanup failure leaves no apparently complete hot docket and is Recorder infrastructure failure.
3. A session/event lifecycle that is itself irreducible proof remains outside generic Recorder retention. The caller creates a specific recording/probe artifact through the affected seam's existing test/fixture lifecycle, subject to CLAUDE graduation/deletion law, and supplies that accepted artifact to the core as an ordinary sealed exhibit. Recorder does not provide a second permanent raw copy.
4. Pre-existing committed artifacts are referenced by verified repository/full-commit/path/blob/SHA-256 coordinates and never copied. Session echoes do not count. New generated Receipts and canonical decision artifacts are stored exactly once by path+digest without claiming a future containing commit. They are not a pending Git reference. The caller commits them; downstream invocations/closure then bind the actual full target HEAD. No self-referential follow-up seal is mandatory merely to restate what Git already records.
5. Mandatory authority/task/input bytes and claim-supporting evidence must be either verified Git references or stored once. They cannot be omitted by disposition. The core manifest is complete only when all such references resolve and all generated artifacts are byte-sealed.
6. Final branch reconstruction preserves the exact small historical Receipts, manifests, authority, reviews, adjudications, plans, and migration dispositions. Existing manifests that mention now-omitted raw payloads remain byte-identical historical artifacts and receive an append-only supersession/disposition; they are not rewritten to look current. The 398 MiB session blobs are intentionally excluded as non-value-bearing execution exhaust after their capacity measurement and accepted Receipts carry the relevant claims.
7. Keep the target outcome measurable: issue #10's reconstructed tracked claim chain should remain on the order of a few MiB, with no generic numeric retention engine. A Plan must report exact reconstructed size and prove no session/tool-event payload entered the branch.

This preserves normal Receipt/audit trust semantics without claiming cryptographic authenticity or independent factual truth. It adds no summarizer, model admission role, cold-store adapter, archive service, LFS, database, catalog, daemon, or automatic Git mutation.
