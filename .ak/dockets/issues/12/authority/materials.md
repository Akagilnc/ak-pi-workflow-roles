# Authority materials for issue #12

Tracker: https://github.com/Akagilnc/ak-pi-workflow-roles/issues/12

## Doctor role: factory health examination and prescription

## Problem

Process fixed cost accretes one-way: every station has case law justifying it, and no station has an exit clause. Evidence in the #12 case file (2026-07-29): issue-granularity disease (#10 carried an epic ~24h against the one-day normal bar), an eight-hole same-wall repair chain, a guardrail complexity cascade priced in repair tickets, and a procedural-refusal tax (spawned #17). Archival memory is complete in dockets, but no seat is tasked with reading it across cases. The factory has gates for every case and no physician for itself.

## Ratified decisions (owner grill, 2026-07-30)

1. **Single patient: the factory** — recorded as ADR 0013; cases carry symptoms only, and in-loop recurrence escalation belongs to #13.
2. **The Doctor is not a work crew** — recorded as ADR 0012; prescriptions enter the normal legal chain, the Doctor stays a permanent evidence seat.
3. **Input boundary.** Readable: the full claim chain (dockets: manifests, Receipts, verdicts, dispositions), Git metadata (commit identity/timestamps/paths), and tracker metadata (issue/PR timestamps). Forbidden: session payloads (same prohibition as Judges), live worktrees, and product source code. When an examination needs deeper evidence, the prescription requests a probe through the normal chain; the Doctor never gathers it itself.
4. **Closure produces the numbers; the Doctor reads them** — recorded as ADR 0014. Stats-line field candidates (role invocation counts, audit rejections, Judge `continue` rounds, first/last manifest timestamps) stay open for authority adjudication below.
5. **Summoning: v1 is hand-summon only.** The owner or the driving caller (ADR 0010) summons on suspicion; trend lines sit on the mandatory closure path, so the trigger is a human glance at data already in the way. No automatic scheduler, cadence, or threshold trigger; a cadence law waits for a real missed-summons failure.
6. **Submission contract.** One typed terminating tool `ak_doctor_output`. Receipt shape: `findings[]`, each carrying the target system artifact, a last-real-bite evidence citation, a disposition recommendation `keep | thin | delete`, and answers to the three guardrail questions; a cross-case trend section; `status: completed | refused` — refusing to examine on insufficient data is a lawful submission. The same Soul-compliance audit as other roles applies; no exemption.
7. **Thin Soul, pointer-cited law.** The three-question guardrail test and the four-reason rejection law are cited from their canonical source, never copied. The Soul's irreducible content: the patient boundary; the examination method — a "real bite" exists only as a dockets Receipt/verdict citation, echoes and prose do not count; and the prescription posture below.
8. **Headline vital sign: issue-open → PR-merged-to-default wall clock,** read as a cross-case population statistic, never a per-case verdict. Reference calibration: one day is normal speed (owner bar).
9. **Prescription taste law: simplification outranks mechanism-addition and patching.** A prescription that adds a mechanism or patch must state why deletion or simplification cannot achieve the same health outcome.
10. **Name: `doctor`.** Deliberately not an authority-organ name; the seat earns trust, not obedience.

## Glossary

- The patient is the factory, never a case.
- Examination covers process, not product.
- Prescriptions treat the system, not symptoms.

## Minimality constraints

- No enforcement power, no gate ownership, no direct law editing, no construction.
- No per-case attendance; the Doctor is out-of-loop by definition.
- No session inspection, no second ledger, no summarizer, no memory of its own beyond what dockets already record.
- No automatic scheduler and no numeric retention/alerting engine; thresholds stay human.
